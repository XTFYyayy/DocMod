《明日方舟》博士mod
=====================================

为《杀戮尖塔2》带来更多角色、卡牌、遗物、时间、怪物等。

模组简介
---------
其实懂的都懂，倒不如在这瞎jb说点啥吧。
塔2上线的那段时间我基本都在塔卫二，塔二和卫。我用了45小时打了全角色的a10，这小研究谁东西的呢，之后就开始寻思把卫扔塔2里面，正好塔2的多人默认的人数上限是4个人。虽然但是现在还没做多人的部分，也没有尝试多人联机。
那么既然是要带一点卫的玩法肯定是要跟阵营有点关系，索性就按照阵营开始做了，一开始做的是卡西米尔，现在是萨尔贡的收尾阶段。其实还算好做，大部分都是重复劳动，毕竟代码里面最多的就是CV。所以说如果有什么文本或者音效特效出了叉子最好也反馈一下。
实习的事现在是有了一些着落，所以之后的制作进度就不一定了——希望不要半途而废吧。
现在的进度是卡牌做了一半不到吧。其他的什么都没做。还有一些观赏性的我也不会整，就比如人物的动作，现在场景里面就是一张图片挂个血条，骨架啥的都没有。
目前可玩角色只有博士，强度还算高，其他的角色暂时不准备提上日程，除非你们能接受一段时间内这些角色都没有任何动作，就像一张贴图——好吧就是贴图。
道阻且长啊。

Doctor Mod (Doc Mod)
=====================================

Machine-translated text following

Adds Arknights characters, cards, relics, events, monsters and more to Slay the Spire 2.

Mod Overview
---------
Those in the know already get it, so I won’t ramble pointlessly here.

Around the launch window of Slay the Spire 2, I was mostly playing Slay the Spire 2 and STRONGHOLD PROTOCOL. I spent 45 hours clearing Ascension 10 with every character, so I know the game pretty well. After that, I started thinking about porting Endfield content into Slay the Spire 2, and coincidentally, the default player cap for multiplayer in Slay the Spire 2 is four people. That said, the multiplayer segment hasn’t been developed yet, and I haven’t tested online co-op at all.

Since the mod will feature gameplay mechanics from STRONGHOLD PROTOCOL, factions naturally became a core design pillar, so I structured development around each faction. I started with Kazimierz, and I’m now wrapping up Sargon’s content. Development itself isn’t overly difficult, though most of the work is repetitive—copy-paste code makes up the bulk of the scripts. If you spot broken text, sound effects or visual effects, please send feedback my way.

My internship plans have partially settled now, so future development progress may slow down—hopefully I won’t abandon this project halfway.

Current progress: less than half of all planned cards are finished. No other content has been implemented yet. I also lack the skills to polish visual presentation, such as character animations. Right now, every character in-game is just a static image with a health bar attached; there are no rigged skeletons or animation frameworks whatsoever.

The only playable character available right now is the Doctor, who has fairly strong power. Other characters are not on the immediate development roadmap unless players are fine with them being purely static sprites with zero animations for an extended period—yes, just flat image assets.

There’s still a long road ahead.

Mod Features
---------
Brand-new character: the Doctor. Additional playable STRONGHOLD PROTOCOL characters including Pepe and Harold will be added later, sharing a unified card pool with the Doctor.
Expanded build archetypes such as Strength and Ingenuity builds
Multiple new HP-restoration cards
